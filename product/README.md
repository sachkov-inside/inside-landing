# Product: Sachkov Inside

Repository-owned product contract for landing and visual-direction work. This file is canonical
for the claims and boundaries used by this repository; unresolved facts stay explicit here rather
than depending on another checkout.

## Definition

**Sachkov Inside** — Kirill Sachkov's authorial engineering membership for
developers who already code and want to stay in demand: modern production and
AI-first practices, sharper engineering skills, better decisions.

Launch format: a monthly Telegram-first subscription — a private channel with
closed materials plus a separate community chat. Later, a dedicated Inside app
becomes the home of materials; Telegram keeps community and announcements.

Working positioning (not the final public offer):

> An authorial engineering membership: the working kitchen of production
> development, AI-first practices and decisions with real artifacts, a
> build-series, and group discussion.

Core of the product — **Kirill's real engineering kitchen**: the decision-making
process with context, trade-offs, mistakes, and artifacts — not ready-made answers.

## Audience

- a developer who already codes (any language);
- wants to grow in web/fullstack engineering, not learn one isolated framework;
- is into backend, frontend, architecture, infrastructure, practical DevOps,
  AI-first workflows;
- wants real engineering decisions with constraints and trade-offs, not
  textbook examples;
- picks their own trajectory — this is not a linear course.

Not for: people starting from zero, or those who need a personal learning path,
homework review, and a guaranteed result by a date.

## What's inside

1. **Closed engineering materials** — posts, guides, breakdowns of production
   approaches, architecture, AI-first work + artifacts (code, diagrams, decision
   documents).
2. **Flagship build-series** — building a real Inside product from scratch inside
   the membership: step videos, guides, artifacts, a versioned live repository
   (read-only for members).
3. **Community** — a separate Telegram chat, Kirill's participation, occasional
   breakdowns and streams. Not unlimited support, not personal mentorship.

## Product character (key input for the visual direction)

- **engineering** — specificity, structure, evidence;
- **alive** — current work, open questions;
- **authorial** — Kirill's recognisable filter and experience, without a
  personality cult;
- **open on the inside** — the member sees deeper than the public post;
- **professional but not corporately cold**;
- **technological but not hype-driven** — AI as practice, not decoration;
- **dense in value, not overloaded**;
- **honest** — boundaries and unfinished states are stated plainly.

## What the product is NOT

- not a linear course, not "access to all courses";
- not personal mentorship or a code-review service;
- not a guarantee of employment or grade;
- not public build-in-public;
- not a library about one language or AI tool.

## Constraints for the landing

The brief deliberately does NOT fix: the final one-sentence promise and CTA,
price and terms, content cadence, logo/palette/typography. That is our working
zone.


Open decisions before the public landing (from the master brief): public promise
formulation, the visitor's main action and CTA, kickoff pack, launch price,
payment provider.
